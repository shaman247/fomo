# Cycle 008 construction studies

Four original equipment scenes in `scene.py` use metres as a common scale; `project.py` derives SVG paths using per-face depth clipping. `views.py` produces front/rear/left/right/top/icon views. The fifth design, gouache, is a flat material-swatch emblem. No third-party artwork, mesh or raster is embedded. Run scripts from the repository root; outputs go to `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-008/`.

- **Cake decorating:** a 30 cm wide illustrative cake on a 37 cm plate with a joined conical decorating bag, nozzle and gathered top. The [Wilton piping demonstration](https://wilton.com/how-to-pipe-a-star/wltech-51/) establishes the upright bag and tip just above the surface. Border dots are simplified frosting deposits rather than exact star-tip grooves. It illustrates equipment use, with no invisible hand implied. The cake size is illustrative rather than an exact rendering of the class's six-inch cake. The initial cream profile was wound inward and its upper surface was culled; reversing that profile restored the intended cap. Lower bead sampling and flat bead paint remove invisible mesh fragmentation.
- **Gouache:** opaque, matte strokes cross a light/dark ground unchanged in color, with a paint tube and brush. This material cue follows [Winsor & Newton's gouache description](https://www.winsornewton.com/collections/designers-gouache). The source does not claim all gouache pigments are equally opaque. This symbol cannot by itself prove medium identity versus acrylic; assignment requires explicit gouache evidence. Paint-tube and brush silhouettes are simple flat emblems, not interlocking equipment.
- **Journaling:** two page blocks over a connected cover, binding, ribbon and a pen resting on the right page. Both body endpoints lie within the leaf and its radius matches the page support offset. A higher orthographic camera and thicker pen improve small-size readability. Personal-heart and ruled-line marks are symbolic, not a branded journal or readable writing.
- **Vintage clothing market:** rack uprights and two stabilizing feet support a joined rail; two hooked triangular hangers support dress shoulders and folded trousers. Both trouser legs have equal length. Collar, belt and flared dress are generic vintage-inspired cues; no exact era or costume is asserted. Blank resale tag is tied to the rail. Clothing structure is simplified into shallow front/back volumes, not a fabric simulation. Garment shopping is the small-size meaning; vintage status comes from the event evidence.
- **Library shelving:** two shelf levels, joined back/side panels and upright books; one pulled-forward book remains substantially supported on the lower board. Upright shelving and proper support follow [Library of Congress guidance](https://guides.loc.gov/preserving-your-books/storing). The pulled-forward item represents active sorting, not recommended permanent storage. The model is not a conservation-storage specification; call numbers and exact collection arrangement are omitted. Reducing spine bands and redundant projected fragments preserves useful form with fewer bytes.

All four cameras and geometry are authoritative. Final native 128/32/24/16 light/dark cards and actual IconManager variants were inspected in a distinct creator critique, not an independent blind study. Paint polygons are united after a 0.01-unit seam closure and simplified at 0.22 viewBox units; the source hash must match the final review. Six-view findings and source hashes are in `geometry-review.json`. Initial/intermediate art, final packets and deterministic gzip measurements are retained in the cycle scratch directory.
