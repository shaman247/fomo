# Batch017 artwork studies

Five original icons cover Punjabi dhol, close-up magic, live tabletop actual play, hanging mobile making and paper gliders. `scene.py` is authoritative for four geometric studies; `close-up-magic.svg` is a planar overhead emblem. No person, licensed game logo, performer likeness or copied photographic geometry appears.

The dhol uses a550mm barrel with330mm heads and a380mm maximum diameter, closed membranes, head hoops and24 alternating rope runs along the exterior. Separate curved/heavier and straight/thinner beaters represent Punjabi playing tools. Decorative tassels connect to ropes. It is an isolated instrument study rather than a supported playing pose. [David Courtney’s construction discussion and Punjabi photograph](https://chandrakantha.com/music-and-dance/instrumental-music/indian-instruments/dhol/) were inspected, including `https://chandrakantha.com/images/dhol/punjabi_dhol.jpg`; [Sonny Singh’s band page](https://sonnysingh.squarespace.com/blo) confirms the event’s dhol feature. The Met’s Bengali dhol record was consulted only as a broad dimensional/material comparison, not a Punjabi construction template. Regional forms vary.

The first dhol SVG was12,810raw/4,633gzip6bytes. Fewer rope samples and one rope paint reduced density. A straight-on vector view exposed a missing strip: independent triangle z-buffer rendering showed intact geometry, and diagnostics traced paint loss to negative polygon buffering. The exporter now computes face-depth planes analytically and uses precision-controlled polygon union without the destructive grow/shrink step. All six views were rechecked; final dhol is8,722raw/3,209gzip6bytes. The change remains local to this prototype exporter.

The mobile uses three equal-area paper leaves, centered equal loads on the lower rail and a calculated top pivot including uniform rod mass. Strings and paint are neglected in the static first-moment check; motion is not simulated. Small first-draft leaves and hairline supports were enlarged/thickened, and the pivot recalculated. [Wave Hill’s paper-leaf program](https://www.wavehill.org/calendar/family-art-project-leafy-mobiles-2) grounds the subject; [Hirshhorn’s maker activity](https://hirshhorn.si.edu/explore/balance-build/) provides construction context without copying its particular artwork.

The hoop glider follows [Exploratorium’s primary construction example](https://annex.exploratorium.edu/science-explorer/hoopster.html): two unequal parallel paper hoops attach to one straight straw. The workshop also names paper airplanes, represented by a separate symmetric folded dart. Reverse faces initially hid it; double-sided panels and distinct fold paints corrected recognition. Neither flight dynamics nor an exact build instruction is claimed. [Walkway’s event](https://walkway.org/event/walkway-connections-for-kids-gliders-and-paper-planes/) confirms both types of experiment.

The actual-play still life combines an equal-edge20-faced die resting on a triangular face, a supported tabletop microphone and a hinged game-master screen. Light die edges were added after the first draft resembled a stone. [Littlefield](https://littlefieldnyc.com/event/?wfea_eb_id=1994519599814) confirms improvised RPG storytelling before spectators. [Urban Illusions](https://urbanillusionsnyc.com/tickets) establishes close-up magic; the planar card/coin mat represents that category without promising an exact routine.

Creator critical review was distinct but not independent or blind. Final native128/32/24/16light/dark cards, runtime theme variants, accepted tabla/RPG/origami/object-handling art and pinned Noto drum/Joker SVGs were inspected. At16px, mobile strings, glider details, dhol tassels and die facets soften; event labels remain necessary. Hash-bound records preserve these limits.
