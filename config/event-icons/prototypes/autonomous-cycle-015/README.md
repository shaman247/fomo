# Batch015 artwork studies

Five original icons cover resin jewelry, coloring supplied pages, Zumba, bookmobile visits and financial coaching. The three planar emblems are authoritative SVGs. `scene.py` holds the authoritative dance figure and original coach adaptation; `project.py` preserves per-face depth occlusion, and `views.py` produces six-view evidence.

The athletic dancer uses fixed paired limb lengths: femurs .420m, tibias .400m, upper arms .285m, forearms .255m. Different foot spacing is solved with inverse kinematics; both soles meet y=0. The upper-arm radius is .039m compared with .071m at the thighs. Skin and clothing form continuous volumes around joints, hands and thumbs connect to wrists, and the short neck overlaps both shirt and head. Skin uses one flat paint throughout. The first pose was revised to lower one bent arm; an inward-facing hair-cap mesh was corrected. Measurements and six-view images are retained in the scratch directory. No exhaustive collision simulation or exercise instruction is claimed.

The bookmobile derives from the accepted original mobile-coach geometry, replacing every medical symbol with an original open-book emblem. It has four equal wheels and connected axles/suspension, continuous body/floor and a closed sloping cab. It is a symbolic library vehicle, not a BPL fleet replica. The resin pendant was changed from a wide shape with an oversized loop to a tall oval and small bail after it resembled a handbag. The financial-coaching checkmark was replaced by a neutral coin to avoid implying a guaranteed outcome.

[Official Zumba Gold format](https://www.zumba.com/en-US/party/classes/class-zumba-gold) supports a grounded generic dance-fitness symbol; the image does not independently identify a brand or imply affiliation. [Arlington Library](https://www.bklynlibrary.org/locations/arlington) confirms bookmobile service during closure. [ArtResin’s primary material example](https://www.artresin.com/blogs/artresin/how-to-make-resin-art-with-dried-flowers) supports embedded dried-flower jewelry; no tutorial sequence, safety advice, logo or image was copied. Pinned Noto adult/front and runner/profile SVGs and accepted Pilates, partner-dance and coach art were inspected as style references.

Creator critique was separate from construction but not independent or blind. Native128/32/24/16light/dark, six geometry views and actual IconManager variants were inspected. Fine face, hand, flower and page detail soften at16px; each review records those limits. Sources stay within the current accepted size range without needing lossy reduction.
