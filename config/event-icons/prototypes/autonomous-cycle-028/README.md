# Batch 28 construction studies

Run scene/project, views and planar_art from repository root. The miniature house and foam blocks use authoritative 3D geometry; three other pictograms are planar SVG. Outputs go to the batch scratch directory.

Original hollow miniature house has approximately 100mm width, 70mm depth, 117mm height above its base, 2mm card walls and folded roof. Surface-painted doors/windows do not imply transparent openings. Roof meets gabled walls; base rests on ground.

Moved the 1mm loose card blank clear of the display base and raised brush center to 4.4mm. Its widest 3.4mm-radius ferrule stays at or above card height. Rechecked front/rear/side/top/icon views. No hand pose or copied construction kit.

Original foam arrangement has a 340mm outer-radius arch, 440mm square block with a 110mm-radius through-hole, and a 550mm loose cylinder. All parts contact y=0 and remain separate; hole is real geometry visible from both ends. Dimensions are pictogram choices, not product specifications.

All geometry retains depth clipping and broad coherent lighting. Sources are 3043/1065 and 1843/826 raw/gzip6 bytes, already within comparable accepted ranges; no global geometry expansion or cosmetic post-projection patch was needed.

Oyster monitoring, musical storytelling and comedy taping are original planar emblems. Final 128/32/24/16px light/dark cards and ten actual IconManager variants inspected against five accepted peers and four pinned Noto references. Distinct creator critical pass, not independent or blind.
