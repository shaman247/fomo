# Batch45 briefs

## Aerial hoop

Lyra/aerial-hoop classes, practice and performances where the suspended circular apparatus is explicit.

Exclusions: Hula hoops, gymnastics rings, generic aerial arts, guaranteed moves or exact rigging equipment.

## Historic cookstove

Demonstrations and historic cooking programs explicitly centered on a wood-fired cookstove.

Exclusions: Modern cooking classes, open-hearth cooking, heating-only stoves or promised participant operation.

## Photography walk

Walks and outings explicitly combining a walking route with taking photographs.

Exclusions: Indoor photo classes, photo editing, ordinary walking without photography, camera-specific requirements or a promised route.

## Pizza making

Hands-on preparing, shaping or topping pizza, including pizza-dough workshops.

Exclusions: Pizza dining, deliveries, general bread making or a specific recipe, oven, topping or dietary claim.

## Play dough

Molding, rolling and shaping soft play dough or modeling compound for creative play.

Exclusions: Edible cooking dough, ceramic firing, polymer-clay jewelry or promised branded products, supplied tools or age suitability.
