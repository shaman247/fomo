# Batch012 artwork studies

Five original SVGs for poetry writing, speed dating, clothing swaps, zine making and captured theater. Full context evidence is frozen for100events196329–196718 in `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-012`.

Four concepts are planar emblems with flat illustrative masks/garments rather than articulated human figures. Their authoritative vector sources are stored here and copied to catalog art. For zine making, `scene.py` is authoritative: run it and `project.py` from the repository root, then `views.py` and the scratch Chromium renderer. The 20cm-wide open booklet consists of joined shallow leaves with explicit thickness, cover and paper layers, attached print graphics, and two staples. The pencil is one collinear assembly beside it. Scale and page curvature are illustrative.

[NYPL’s zine overview](https://www.nypl.org/node/264791) describes varied handmade publications including stapled forms. [CUNY’s making guide](https://openlab.bmcc.cuny.edu/bmcc-zine-library/how-to-make-a-zine/) describes stapled booklet construction. These ground a representative booklet, not a promised technique at the reviewed workshop. No source artwork, text layout or images were copied. Flat motifs were compared with pinned Noto memo, T-shirt, performing-arts masks and stopwatch, plus accepted custom journal, bookbinding, poetry-reading, vintage-clothing and opera-screening art.

Creator critique is automated but neither independent nor blind. First-pass clothing arrows incorrectly pointed to the same garment; the lower arrow now returns to the upper shirt. The first zine projection hid most of the pencil and omitted finite page thickness; closed thin leaves and an explicit adjacent pencil now survive geometry inspection. Front, rear, left, right, top and icon views were inspected. Final native128/32/24/16light/dark cards and actual IconManager renders were inspected. See the hash-bound review JSON for precise limitations and raw/gzip6 measurements. No exhaustive collision or physical-material simulation is claimed.
