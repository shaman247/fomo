# Batch022 briefs

## Smocking

Gathering and reshaping fabric with decorative hand stitches, including punk smocking.

Exclude: Flat embroidery without gathers, ordinary mending, knitting or a promised exact smocking pattern.

## Sun printing

Creating photographic or light-sensitive prints by exposing treated paper or fabric to sunlight.

Exclude: Ordinary photography, painted botanical pictures, darkroom-only photograms or a promise of cyanotype chemistry or exact colors.

## Darkroom photograms

Camera-less darkroom photographic printing with objects and shadows on light-sensitive material.

Exclude: Lens-camera photography, digital editing, ordinary drawings, sun-printing programs with their own icon or a fixed choice of objects.

## Sketch comedy

Shows and workshops explicitly centered on prepared comic sketches and scripted comic scenes.

Exclude: Unscripted improv, solo stand-up, non-comic plays or generic mixed comedy without sketch focus.

## Terrarium making

Creating, planting or arranging a miniature garden in a transparent terrarium container.

Exclude: General outdoor gardening, greenhouse tours, opaque flowerpots or a promised sealed ecosystem, plant species or vessel shape.
