# Autonomous cycle 010 equipment studies

Four original 3D equipment diagrams produce wet felting, sushi making, pond/stream dipping and cider-tasting SVGs. Comic drawing is a directly drawn planar page-and-pencil emblem. Run `scene.py`, `project.py` and `views.py` from the repository root; outputs go into `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-010/`. `scene.py` is authoritative for spatial revisions; `project.py` clips faces according to depth and merges simplified opaque paint regions.

Structural references:

- [Kikkoman makisu glossary](https://www.kikkoman.com/en/cookbook/glossary/makisu.html): bamboo strips joined by perpendicular thread; common24–30cm size and rice/nori rolling. The study uses a28×24cm mat, parallel roll axis/slats, and visible filling at both roll ends. No source artwork is embedded.
- [Ashford, The Wheel32](https://www.ashford.co.nz/images/download_pdfs/wheel_magazine/the_wheel_magazine_32.pdf): wetting wool with soapy water, rubbing and rolling. Wool pad, working mat, soap and water emblem identify the process; particular workshop tools are not promised.
- [RSPB Leighton Moss pond dipping](https://group.rspb.org.uk/leightonmosswex/news-blogs/blog/pond-dipping/): dip nets and pond-water observation trays establish the equipment combination. Hoop mesh, socket, pole and grip form one connected tool. The net is shown poised beside the tray as an equipment diagram, with no implied invisible supporting person.

Dimensions are illustrative. Glassware uses a closed profile around a smaller liquid volume, plus a surface-aligned colored overlay to communicate transparency in opaque vector paint. It does not simulate refraction or specify a sample count. The three glass bottoms and apple base meet the flight board. The apple cue also covers pear-cider tasting as a beverage family; it is not an ingredient guarantee.

Creator critique revised the wool marks from an X to tapered wisps; increased mat slat thickness and simplified their shading; exposed both sushi roll ends; aligned cider overlays to the glass facets, reduced liquid radius to fit inside, and lowered the apple onto the board. A second pass thickened the net hoop/mesh for dark-background legibility and moved the tray4cm forward to clear the tool grip. Native-size contact sheets, real IconManager light/dark rendering, and front/rear/left/right/top/icon views are saved in the cycle scratch directory. Final hash-specific findings are in `geometry-review.json` and the cycle visual-review records.
