# Batch 38 briefs

## Early-childhood music

Participatory infant, toddler and preschool music programs centered on singing, rhythm play or music-led movement.

Exclusions: General early learning with incidental songs, ordinary storytime, adult singalongs, child concerts without participation, or a promise of particular instruments.

## Artist talk

Artists, composers, directors and other creative practitioners discussing their own artistic work, ideas or process.

Exclusions: Author book conversations, historical lectures about absent artists, participant artmaking, studio visits, or a promise of performance excerpts.

## Music jam

Participatory collaborative music jams and open improvisational music making.

Exclusions: Prepared concerts, comedy improv, karaoke, rap cyphers, a fixed instrument lineup or guaranteed supplied equipment.

## Rap cypher

Collaborative freestyle rap cyphers where participants practice and exchange improvised rap.

Exclusions: Competitive rap or beat battles, spoken poetry, prepared rap concerts, or assumptions about recorded versus live beats or microphones supplied.

## Preschool multisport

Toddler and preschool programs explicitly introducing multiple sports through age-appropriate play.

Exclusions: One-sport training, adult fitness, general motor-learning play without sports, or a promise of a specific ball, equipment set or sport rotation.
