# Batch020 briefs

## Hand stamping

Making patterns or impressions by hand with rubber stamps and an ink pad, on paper, fabric or other craft surfaces.

Exclude: Letterpress, postage collecting, machine printing, stamp-carving without printing or an exact stamp design.

## Cardboard construction

Building and transforming cardboard into play structures, models, props or imaginative constructions.

Exclude: Ordinary packaging, cardboard recycling without making, wood construction, finished architecture tours or a specific model scale.

## Ravioli

Making, shaping or tasting ravioli where filled pasta is central.

Exclude: Spaghetti, generic pasta events without ravioli, other dumpling traditions or a promised filling, shape or serving sauce.

## Chalk drawing

Participatory chalk drawing and decorating chalkboards or pavement with chalk.

Exclude: Pastel-paper technique, wax crayon coloring, generic drawing, classroom lectures or viewing finished chalk murals without drawing.

## Erasure poetry

Writing poetry by selecting and removing, obscuring or rearranging words from an existing text, including blackout-poetry workshops.

Exclude: Generic new-poem writing, administrative document redaction, censorship reports, erasing drawing mistakes or a fixed erasure method promised by the icon.
