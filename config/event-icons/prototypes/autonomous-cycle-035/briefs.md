# Batch35 briefs

## Needlelace

Needle-and-thread lace making, including European needlelace frameworks and filler stitches.

Exclusions: Shuttle tatting, bobbin lace, crochet, ordinary surface embroidery or a promise of a particular finished pattern.

## Acrylic painting

Painting instruction and hands-on projects explicitly centered on acrylic paint.

Exclusions: Oil, watercolor or gouache without acrylic focus, viewing finished paintings, or a specific paint package or brush promise.

## Oil painting

Oil-painting classes, practice and demonstrations where oils are the billed focus, including sessions allowing other media.

Exclusions: Generic painting, oil pastels, culinary oil, finished-art exhibitions or a guaranteed palette-knife technique.

## Emergency preparedness

Programs teaching household or community emergency planning and preparedness.

Exclusions: An actual emergency, medical treatment, rescue response, ordinary backpacking or a complete supply checklist.

## Bar crawl

Organized social visits to several bars, pubs or drinking establishments.

Exclusions: Single-venue parties, food walking tours, guaranteed alcoholic drinks or a specific route, venue count or admission inclusion.
