# Batch 30 briefs

## Language learning

Structured spoken/written language courses teaching literacy, vocabulary, grammar and comprehension.

Exclusions: Reciprocal language exchanges, conversation-only practice, sign-language gestures, literary history courses or a particular alphabet/national identity.

## Ghost tour

Guided tours of ghost lore, haunted histories and places, outdoors or within a historic building.

Exclusions: Horror film screenings, unguided haunted attractions, paranormal facts or a promised appearance of spirits.

## Forest bathing

Guided or dedicated mindful sensory immersion in woodland or a natural setting.

Exclusions: Literal bathing, swimming, strenuous hiking, tree-care volunteering or promised medical treatment.

## Victorian hairwork

Exhibitions, study or making of decorative hairwork, including historical hair wreaths and keepsakes.

Exclusions: Haircuts, hairstyling, ordinary floral wreaths or assuming all hairwork commemorates the dead.

## Fundraising gala

Events explicitly billed as benefit or fundraising galas supporting an organization or cause.

Exclusions: All benefit concerts, casual donation drives, weddings, ordinary dinners or a particular dress code, food or auction promise.
