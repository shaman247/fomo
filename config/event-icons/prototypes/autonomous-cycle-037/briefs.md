# Batch 37 briefs

## Performance and discussion

Programs combining live performance excerpts or demonstrations with a substantial discussion by artists or the creative team.

Exclusions: Full performances without a stated discussion, film Q&A, ordinary lectures, or a promise of complete staging or audience participation.

## Fragrance appreciation

Guided perfume or scent appreciation, smelling exercises and cultural study of fragrance.

Exclusions: Perfume formulation or blending, aromatherapy treatment, general flowers, or a promise of particular fragrances or testing materials.

## Grant application help

Clinics and workshops about funding eligibility, grant applications, proposals and related questions.

Exclusions: Guaranteed funding, awards, personal financial coaching, startup pitch contests or a claim of professional legal representation.

## Focaccia

Focaccia baking, preparation and programs explicitly centered on the bread, including edible focaccia decoration.

Exclusions: Generic bread, pizza, cake decoration, or a promise of a specific topping arrangement, recipe or dietary suitability.

## Seining

Nature programs and activities explicitly using a seine net to collect or observe aquatic life.

Exclusions: Angling, dip-net pond study, trawling or purse-seine operations, guaranteed catches or species, or a precise deployment instruction.
