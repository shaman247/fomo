# Batch019 briefs

## Prize wheel

Events explicitly centered on spinning a prize wheel, including live gameshows with audience wheel participation.

Exclude: Ferris wheels, roulette, general trivia, ordinary raffles or a replica of a particular branded stage game.

## Fascinator making

Making or reshaping small sculptural cocktail hats, fascinators and perchers, including draped finished felt.

Exclude: Generic hat wearing, top-hat costume parties, full-size brimmed hat blocking, or wet/needle felting without a fascinator project.

## Duct-tape crafts

Hands-on craft projects explicitly constructed with duct tape, including portable carriers and other folded/taped objects.

Exclude: Ordinary repairs, packaging tape, electrical tape, sewing or a specific finished project not established by the event.

## Pressed flower crafts

Arranging or preserving flattened flowers/leaves in bookmarks, cards or other pressed-botanical crafts.

Exclude: Fresh flower arranging, floral painting, flower rescue, seed paper or live plant identification.

## Handmade cards

Making, painting or decorating greeting cards and handmade paper greetings.

Exclude: Playing cards, business-card networking, sending ready-made mail, general painting without a card project or a fixed holiday theme.
