# Batch 41 briefs

## Coworking

Shared sessions centered on participants doing their own work alongside others, including optional accountability huddles and demos.

Exclusions: Networking-only mixers, employer office visits, computer instruction, a guaranteed job or supplied equipment.

## Academic exam preparation

Instruction and practice explicitly preparing students for academic admissions or school examinations.

Exclusions: Taking the official exam, citizenship testing, general homework help, guaranteed scores or admission, or an exact paper/computer testing format.

## Record fair

Markets and fairs centered on browsing and buying recorded music, especially vinyl, from multiple dealers or collectors.

Exclusions: DJ performances alone, passive listening, record-release concerts, equipment sales or a guaranteed recording format or inventory.

## Flea market

Multi-vendor markets mixing secondhand, vintage, antique or collectible goods, sometimes alongside handmade goods.

Exclusions: Single-household yard sales, clothing-only resale, makers-only fairs, produce markets or a promise of any pictured item.

## Architecture walking tour

Guided walks interpreting buildings, architectural history and traces of past structures in the landscape.

Exclusions: Generic sightseeing, artist studio visits, film-location walks, construction work, guaranteed interior access or official heritage designation.
