# Batch 42 briefs

## Subway tour

Guided tours exploring subway stations and transit history through walking or subway travel.

Exclusions: Ordinary commuting, model railways, aboveground railway excursions, closed-track access or guaranteed vintage train rides.

## Zine fair

Fairs and markets centered on independent zines, small-press publications and their creators.

Exclusions: Zine-making workshops, ordinary book reading, commercial bookstore browsing or guaranteed free publications.

## Shadow theater

Performances using projected shadows of puppets, drawings or found objects as their central theatrical medium.

Exclusions: Live cinema shadowcasts, hand/glove puppets, ordinary film projection or a promise of a particular shadow subject or puppet mechanism.

## Playwriting

Participatory writing, devising and adaptation exercises centered on scripts for live theater.

Exclusions: Screenplay-only instruction, watching a play, reciting a finished script, general prose writing or guaranteed production of the participant’s work.

## Oyster research tank

Classroom aquarium setup, care and research explicitly centered on live oysters and monitoring their tank environment.

Exclusions: Field oyster-monitoring stations, oyster meals, ordinary pet fishkeeping, a complete installation guide or exact supplied tank inventory.
