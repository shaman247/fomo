# Batch 40 briefs

## Spelling bee

Spelling competitions and spelling-bee gameshows, including comic variants with participant word spelling.

Exclusions: Ordinary literacy lessons, trivia without spelling, insect programs or a guaranteed stage slot or prize.

## Expert interview

Live interviews in which a host questions a specialist about a defined field or topic.

Exclusions: An artist discussing only their own creative work, author book conversations, monologue lectures, recorded podcasts without a live interview, or verified truth of every speaker claim.

## Pet costume contest

Pet costume contests and costume parades where dressed pets are the central participants.

Exclusions: Human-only costume parties, ordinary dog walks, pet adoption, animal handling instructions or guaranteed prizes.

## Divination pendulum

Workshops and activities explicitly centered on a suspended pendulum used as a divination or reflective spiritual tool.

Exclusions: Clockwork pendulums, laboratory measurement, general crystal shopping, reliable prediction, medical diagnosis or a claim of verified supernatural effects.

## Candle carving

Hands-on carving, inscribing and decorating wax candles, including ritual craft workshops.

Exclusions: Ordinary candle lighting, candle pouring alone, generic drawing or promises of spell results, healing or particular supplied tools.
