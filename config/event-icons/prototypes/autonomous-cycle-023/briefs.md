# Batch023 briefs

## Shuttle tatting

Making knotted lace with a tatting shuttle, including rings, chains and picots.

Exclude: Needle tatting without a shuttle, crochet, ordinary embroidery or a guaranteed exact lace motif.

## Stuffed appliqué

Creating stuffed or padded appliqué and traditional trapunto with raised fabric shapes.

Exclude: Flat appliqué, ordinary mending, finished-quilt exhibitions or a promised exact pattern.

## English paper piecing

Hand piecing fabric around removable paper templates for quilt patterns.

Exclude: Paper origami, foundation piecing without wrapped templates, machine-only quilt assembly or a fixed triangle pattern.

## Bargello embroidery

Counted canvas embroidery with vertical stitches arranged in stepped colored patterns, explicitly identified as Bargello.

Exclude: Ordinary flat stitch patterns, knitted zigzags, Bargello quilt piecing or a promised historic pattern.

## Digital music production

Creating, arranging, processing or finishing musical tracks in a digital audio workstation, including Ableton workshops.

Exclude: Passive electronic concerts, DJ sets, podcast-only editing, acoustic composition without software or a promised software version.
