# Batch023 construction studies

Authoritative `scene.py`, `project.py` and `views.py` preserve the shuttle and padded-textile geometry. Other icons are direct planar symbols; reproducible source is preserved in `planar_art.py` and the three SVGs. No third-party mesh or artwork is embedded.

Original60mm by24mm post shuttle has two shell plates, an internal post and wound thread. Plates approach and meet at closed points. Side gap and post support are visible in front/rear/side views. Post endpoint was lowered below upper shell to remove an accidental visible dot. Lace rings and picots are enlarged technique symbols, with joins represented but not individual double knots or a working lace pattern.

Original180x170mm intact foundation supports a124x92mm oval pad rising25mm. Representative joining stitches pass through pad edges into the foundation. Six views establish padded volume and support; broad shading follows the same light direction. No stuffing simulation or exact workshop design is claimed.

English paper piecing is a planar symbolic arrangement of joined fabric triangles, a reverse-side paper-backed triangle and hand needle. TATTER materials establish triangle templates; the icon does not copy the pictured quilt layout or promise that exact pattern.

Bargello is an original planar four-color stepped pattern of vertical stitch bars, with a needle. This represents counted canvas embroidery rather than knitted fabric or Bargello quilt-strip piecing. Fine thread texture is omitted.

Digital music production is a planar application symbol: original track blocks, timeline playhead, transport triangle and two mixer sliders. Ableton manual text and screenshot support this visual vocabulary. No vendor interface, logo or software version is reproduced.

Current source hashes were inspected at128/32/24/16px on light/dark, with ten actual IconManager variants and accepted embroidery/quilting/applique/audio-editing plus pinned Noto thread/needle/laptop/music-note references. Fine textile details and software specificity depend on labels at16px. Review is by the creator, not independent or blind.
