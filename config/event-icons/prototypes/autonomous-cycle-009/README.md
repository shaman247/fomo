# Cycle 009 construction studies

Four original equipment scenes in `scene.py` use metres as a common scale. `project.py` derives vector paths with per-face depth clipping; `views.py` renders front/rear/left/right/top/icon views. Author conversation is a simple planar book-and-dialogue emblem. No third-party artwork or raster is embedded. Run from the repository root; evidence goes to `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-009/`.

- **Live film score:** freestanding screen with foreground score desk, tripod mast, explicit mounting bracket and a baton resting along its ledge. A large beamed note replaces tiny notation that looked like classroom notes. The [official ATG concert page](https://us.atgtickets.com/events/my-hero-academia/kings-theatre-brooklyn/) confirms live music paired with visuals. One symbolic music stand does not specify ensemble size or instruments. Film scenery is generic.
- **Outdoor cinema:** same coherent screen construction, lawn plane, two empty folding chairs and a crescent sky emblem. Chair side frames cross and support seat/back; the final lawn meets the feet. The design depicts an outdoor screening setup, not an actual venue or weather forecast. Fine frame details may soften at16px.
- **Mobile clinic:** generic clinic coach with a closed cab, surface-mounted windshield/headlamps, equal wheels, joined axles/suspension and narrowed chassis. The body clears the wheel tops. [NYP's program description](https://www.nyp.org/acn/community-programs/sexual-health-mmu) establishes the traveling clinic format; [LDV's platform descriptions](https://www.ldvusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/gsa_for-web_ssv.pdf) document medical vehicles on bus and cab/chassis platforms. The six-metre body study is deliberately a compact illustrative vehicle, not a replica of either provider's equipment. Detailed photographic reference could not be retrieved; no claim of exact vehicle fidelity is made. It has no siren or emergency lighting and makes no claim about specific treatments, accessibility hardware or interior rooms.
- **Mural painting:** solid masonry wall and painted image with a roller poised5mm in front of its painted surface. The cover, bent shank and handle are joined; [Wooster's roller-frame description](https://www.woosterbrush.com/roller-frames/sherlock-frames/) grounds the roller/cage, angled shank and grip arrangement. Roller width and shank thickness are exaggerated for icon scale, not manufacturing dimensions. A trial paint swatch merged with the roller, so it was removed. Rose paint, a thicker exposed frame and a short overhang make the tool clearer; fewer masonry courses reduce vector weight. The particular mural and roller are representative, not promised attendee materials.
- **Author conversation:** an open book beneath two opposing speech bubbles. It expresses literary dialogue without drawing an exact author, audience count, lectern or venue. Explicit author/book context is required; it is not a participant book-club or reading-recital icon.

The scene remains authoritative for pose/layout and occlusion. All final 128/32/24/16 light/dark cards and actual IconManager variants received a distinct creator critical pass, not independent blind evaluation. Geometry checks cover observed joins, clearances and support points; they do not constitute exhaustive collision testing or engineering certification. Final hashes, byte comparisons, limitations and revisions are recorded in `geometry-review.json` and the cycle's versioned visual records.
