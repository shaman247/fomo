# Batch 44 briefs

## Sensory bin

Participatory tactile exploration, sorting and scooping in a sensory bin or tray.

Exclusions: Ordinary gardening, archaeological excavation, cooking, medical sensory therapy or a guaranteed filler and age suitability.

## Foil sculpture

Hands-on shaping, folding or crumpling thin metallic craft foil into sculptural forms.

Exclusions: Goldsmithing, precious-metal investment, gold-leaf gilding, foil printing or a specific cultural artifact or material purity.

## Finger puppets

Making or performing with small puppets fitted over individual fingers.

Exclusions: Full-hand glove puppets, marionettes, rod puppets, shadow theater or a promised character/material pattern.

## Interview practice

Coaching and rehearsal for job or informational interviews, including mock interviews and response practice.

Exclusions: Actual recruitment interviews, expert stage interviews, podcast recording, resume-only review or guaranteed hiring.

## Personal statement writing

Writing, revising and receiving feedback on personal statements for applications and related self-narrative exercises.

Exclusions: Resume/CV listings, general memoir writing, graduate fairs, admissions decisions or guaranteed acceptance.
