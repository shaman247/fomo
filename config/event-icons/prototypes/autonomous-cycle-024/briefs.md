# Batch024 briefs

## Sound bath

Immersive sound-bath sessions centered on listening and relaxation, with any supported mix of sound sources.

Exclude: Ordinary concerts, sound engineering, generic yoga or meditation, a promised instrument, posture or health outcome.

## Herbal preparation

Hands-on making or blending herbal preparations, including take-home herbal products.

Exclude: Passive herbalism lectures, general botany, prescribed medication, a particular recipe or any efficacy/safety promise.

## Garden stewardship

Hands-on garden or urban-farm tending that combines planting, soil work, harvesting and maintenance.

Exclude: Garden viewing, botanical art, animal-only care or a promise of exact tools, plants or raised beds.

## Truth-or-lie storytelling

Storytelling games in which listeners identify a fictional story among purportedly true accounts.

Exclude: Personal storytelling without a guessing game, ordinary trivia, factual lectures or a guarantee of four stories.

## Classical piano trio

Chamber programs centered on a billed violin, cello and piano lineup.

Exclude: Jazz piano/bass/drums trio, three unspecified performers, a sole featured soloist or a promised complete concert layout.
