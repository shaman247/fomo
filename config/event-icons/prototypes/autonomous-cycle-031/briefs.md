# Batch31 briefs

## Stage combat

Unarmed stage-combat classes and theatrical fight choreography.

Exclusions: Real fighting, armed fencing, martial-arts competition or an instructional fighting diagram.

## 35mm screening

Screenings explicitly presented on 35mm film.

Exclusions: Digital cinema,70mm or unspecified projection formats.

## Mandolin

Mandolin lessons and explicitly mandolin-led performances or ensembles.

Exclusions: Banjo, ukulele, guitar, incidental mandolin in a mixed band or a promised model.

## Picture framing

Making and assembling custom picture frames, including woodworking frame classes.

Exclusions: Gallery viewing, general carpentry or a specific joint/tool technique not stated by the event.

## Blood pressure screening

Blood-pressure measurement, screening and cuff-equipment demonstrations.

Exclusions: Blood donation, ECG-only programs, a particular device brand, numerical medical reading or promised diagnosis.
