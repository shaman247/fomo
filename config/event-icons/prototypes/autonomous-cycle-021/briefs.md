# Batch021 briefs

## Monopoly

Playing the named Monopoly property-trading board game.

Exclude: Generic board games, real-estate classes, Monopoly-themed talks without play or a promised edition.

## Resume coaching

Individual or workshop assistance creating, reviewing and improving a resume or CV.

Exclude: Generic job fairs, interviews without resume feedback, business-plan coaching or guaranteed employment.

## Tech help

Hands-on assistance learning to use everyday devices, software or online services.

Exclude: Hardware repair, coding classes, specific software training without individual help or guaranteed support for every device.

## Studio recording

Participatory audio recording sessions in a studio, including recording music or spoken work with technical assistance.

Exclude: A concert, listening party, generic music class, equipment-only tour or an exact microphone or studio package.

## Party cruise

Boat or yacht cruises centered on a dance party, DJ, live music or festive nightlife.

Exclude: Ordinary sightseeing ferries, sailing lessons, shore parties, guaranteed headliner attendance or an exact vessel.
