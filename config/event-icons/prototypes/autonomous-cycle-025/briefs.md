# Batch 25 briefs

## Astrology

Astrology workshops, birth-chart interpretation and explicitly astrological lunar or zodiac sessions.

Exclusions: Astronomy, planetarium visits, general spiritual practice without astrology, a real calculated chart or predictions.

## Songwriting

Participatory songwriting, lyric and melody composition, and collaborative song-creation sessions.

Exclusions: Singer-songwriter concerts, poetry alone, ordinary jam sessions, guaranteed notation instruction or a specific instrument.

## Junk journaling

Assembling or decorating personal junk journals with found papers, tickets, fabric and recycled scraps.

Exclusions: Writing-only journaling, general collage without a journal, binding-only classes or a promised exact project.

## Menstrual products

Menstrual-product distribution, period pantries and supply-focused programs.

Exclusions: General wellness, blood donation, a particular product brand or a guarantee of pad/tampon availability.

## Digital sculpting

Creating and shaping virtual three-dimensional forms with digital sculpting tools.

Exclusions: Physical clay work, 3D printing without sculpting, ordinary 2D drawing or a promised software/hardware setup.
