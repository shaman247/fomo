# Batch 27 briefs

## Structured debate

Formal debates with designated speakers arguing opposing positions.

Exclusions: General panel discussions, informal conversation, arguments or a promised venue setup.

## Community event planning

Learning or practicing event planning, including volunteer coordination, promotion and resources.

Exclusions: Attending an ordinary event, generic calendar entries or business meetings without planning evidence.

## Low garden tunnel

Constructing or using low arched crop covers for garden season extension.

Exclusions: Walk-in greenhouses, tunnels for people, ordinary planting or a promised construction kit.

## Rain gardens

Learning, making or stewarding rain gardens that collect stormwater in planted soil basins.

Exclusions: Ordinary watering, ponds, all gardening or an engineering design/construction specification.

## Seed collecting

Collecting plant seeds for propagation, conservation or restoration.

Exclusions: Seed viewing alone, sowing-only classes, produce harvest or implied permission to collect wild plants.
