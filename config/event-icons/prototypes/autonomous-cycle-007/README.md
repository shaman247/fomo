Meal packing does not claim a particular cuisine or container, and community meal icons represent the volunteer context established by the event. Unicode fallback values remain intact.

`scene.py` defines four authoritative equipment studies in metres; `project.py` clips faces against projected depth, unions paint and simplifies contours to 0.16 viewBox units. `views.py` generates front, rear, left, right, top and icon views. `flat_art.py` produces the planar genealogy record diagram. Run these with the project venv from the repository root; output goes to `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-007/art/`.

Meal-tray base, walls, divider and rear hinge join. Food stays inside the tray. Stockpot outer and inner walls close at the floor; handles join and contents lie below the rim. Board, knife and both carrot endpoints have explicit support contacts. The serving bowl has a closed floor and hollow rim; the connected ladle rests inside and across the right rim. Containers, utensils and food are illustrative, not exact products or meal promises. No humans or hands are modeled.

Goalball construction was checked against the [IBSA 2025 rules, section 4.1](https://www.usaba.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-IBSA-Goalball-Rules-and-Regulations-v2-1.pdf) and [IBSA overview](https://ibsasport.org/sports/goalball/about/overview/). The blue ball is 24 cm across and has eight actual mesh apertures in two staggered hemispheric rings. A dark interior surface stands for the unseen hollow interior; the two internal bells are hidden. Apertures are mildly enlarged for legibility, and knobbed microtexture is omitted. The eyeshades have an opaque curved face, nose notch and connected rear elastic band. This is representative equipment, not manufacturing geometry. No third-party image, mesh, logo or texture was copied.

The initial tray looked empty, so lid height and rim weight were reduced, the camera raised, and food paint clarified. Tapered carrot tips initially hovered and now meet the board. The meal-service handle was shortened and its camera raised to keep the bowl legible. The goalball holes were staggered to match the rule and mesh density increased to reduce coarse boundaries. A family-heart card distinguishes the genealogy graph from a generic organization chart.

See `geometry-review.json` and the [hash-bound final review](../../reviews/20260909-autonomous-cycle-007.json). Review was a distinct creator critical pass, not independent/blind testing. Native 128/32/24/16 px cards were inspected on light/dark beside accepted food-distribution, weeding and cocktail-mixing artwork; ten actual IconManager variants decoded and reused their caches. Fine utensil/record/hole details soften at 16 px. The equipment and primary silhouettes remain clearest at 24/32 px.
