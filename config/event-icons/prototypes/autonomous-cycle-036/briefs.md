# Batch 36 briefs

## Concert film

Cinema presentations of recorded concert performances, including restored or filmed concert events.

Exclusions: Live music performed in the room, a live score accompanying a film, music-video DJ sets, ordinary music-themed fiction or guaranteed live performer attendance.

## Escape room

Team escape-room puzzle adventures with clues, riddles and a game facilitator.

Exclusions: Actually locking people in a room, an emergency exit, ordinary jigsaw play, unsupervised escape attempts or a promise of a specific room mechanism.

## Food truck event

Food truck gatherings, mobile-kitchen festivals and events explicitly focused on food trucks.

Exclusions: Ordinary delivery trucks, a stationary restaurant, guaranteed food availability, free meals or a particular vendor’s vehicle.

## Fly tying

Classes, demonstrations and practice assembling fishing flies with hooks, thread and tying materials.

Exclusions: Outdoor fishing itself, generic sewing, guaranteed hands-on participation or supplied kits, or one required fly pattern or vise model.

## Singalong

Programs centered on participants singing along together, including singalong brunches and audience song sessions.

Exclusions: Formal choirs, karaoke without evidence, ordinary concerts with only incidental audience songs, or a guaranteed lyric screen or song list.
