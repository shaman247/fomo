# Continuous backlog batch 011 studies

Four original 3D scenes produce artist-studio access, outdoor sculpture viewing, traditional-climbing protection and special-waste collection. Urban sketching is a directly drawn planar sketchbook-and-pencil emblem. `scene.py` is authoritative; `project.py` retains per-face depth occlusion and simplifies/merges opaque paint. `views.py` renders front, rear, left, right, top and icon views into the cycle scratch directory.

Structural and semantic references:

- [Black Diamond C4 product page](https://blackdiamondequipment.com/products/c4-camalot) and its manufacturer photo of size3 were visually inspected in Chrome. The [manufacturer document](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/catsy.865/13747_G_Camalot_C4-ULC4_IS-WEB.pdf) and product description identify the double-axle construction, cable loop/stem, pull trigger and sewn sling. Four separate lobe plates, two axles, four trigger wires and those connected components are modeled. The generic solid lobe contours omit manufacturing cutouts and do not reproduce a rated logarithmic cam profile. The unplaced assembly is rotated45degrees in the icon camera plane for better use of a square canvas. No climbing placement, rope arrangement, product rating or instruction is represented.
- [DSNY SAFE disposal](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/what-we-do/programs/safe-disposal-events.page) describes dedicated harmful-household-product collection and lists all three cohort sites. This grounds the collection crate, sealed can and battery. The small caution triangle is a generic identifier, not a hazard classification or packing instruction. Accepted products must come from the actual program; the illustration does not promise universal acceptance.
- [ASLA urban sketch walk](https://www.aslany.org/event/sketching-the-city/) establishes observational drawing of public landscapes, with the listed Wagner Park meeting point recorded as a separate metadata lead. The skyline drawing is city-neutral.

The studio has a connected door frame, a door rotating about the left hinge and an easel with three legs, shelf and rear brace. The canvas bottom meets its shelf. This is a representative studio, not a replica or a promise that every artist works in paint. The sculpture is an invented continuous elliptical loop supported by a plinth; the adjacent path/tree establish outdoor viewing. It does not copy a named artist’s work.

Creator critique removed the sculpture’s false segmented seams by replacing capped tube segments with one continuous mesh, rounded the tree silhouette, aligned the can lid with the sealed vessel and fitted the warning motif within the crate wall. Rotating the complete cam study improved canvas use without changing component relationships. Final hash-bound critique and six-view observations are recorded in `geometry-review.json` and the cycle visual JSON. Fine wires, easel hardware, drawing windows and warning punctuation soften at16px; the cam is a specialist equipment cue best read with event context.

Evidence: `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-011/` contains source packets, three visual stages, full-scene views and actual IconManager light/dark rendering. All geometry is original. Reference images and manufacturer artwork are not embedded in the SVGs. Dimensions and shapes are illustrative, not fabrication or engineering specifications.
