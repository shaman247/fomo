# Batch013 artwork studies

Five original SVGs represent recorded-music listening circles, comedy roast battles, museum object handling, wooden furniture making, and participant small-group discussion. Three are direct planar symbols, kept here as authoritative vectors. `scene.py` is authoritative for the stool/tools and camera/material tray. Run `scene.py`, `project.py` and `views.py` from the repository root to regenerate the original mesh evidence and derived SVGs. The scratch renderers produce native-size review and geometry sheets.

The stool has four equal legs, connected apron/low rails, and a seat whose bottom meets the leg tops. A mallet rests on the seat; its handle is embedded in the head. A separate chisel has connected handle, ferrule and beveled blade. The handling tray has joined edges and a mat supporting a generic camera and material tile. Camera lens cylinders are coaxial; its viewfinder and shutter are attached. A supported sign carries a flat five-digit handprint. There are no articulated human figures.

[Preservation Long Island’s primary event page](https://preservationlongisland.org/historic-furniture-making-demonstration/) confirms historic furniture joinery, antique hand tools and participatory demonstrations. The icon uses a generic wooden stool and tools; it does not reproduce or promise a named historical chair or workshop project. [MoMI’s primary page](https://movingimage.org/event/touch-object-experience/2026-09-26/) describes facilitated handling of selected cameras and other film-related objects/materials. The camera and material tile are representative of that broader format. No source photographs, named artifacts, logos or third-party meshes were copied.

Creator critique was separate from drawing but was not independent or blind. Front, rear, left, right, top and icon projections were inspected for connections, shared support planes, attached markings and depth order. No material defect was found in the first native128/32/24/16light/dark pass; the final source was rerendered with the same hashes, plus actual IconManager variants. Fine grooves, handprint fingers and tool details soften at16px, as recorded in each final review. Sizes are within the accepted custom range without needing lossy reduction. No exhaustive collision, historical-replica or fabrication validation is claimed.
