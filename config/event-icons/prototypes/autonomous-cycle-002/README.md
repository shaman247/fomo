# Cycle 002 icon construction

Original art for collaborative painting, backlit guided painting, needle felting, recreational lockpicking and community repair. `scene.py` defines two spatial craft scenes; `project.py` clips projected mesh faces by relative depth and merges paint. `flat_art.py` draws three simple planar diagrams. No human anatomy or external artwork is used.

Run from repository root with the existing venv:

```sh
./venv/bin/python config/event-icons/prototypes/autonomous-cycle-002/scene.py
./venv/bin/python config/event-icons/prototypes/autonomous-cycle-002/views.py
./venv/bin/python config/event-icons/prototypes/autonomous-cycle-002/project.py
./venv/bin/python config/event-icons/prototypes/autonomous-cycle-002/flat_art.py
```

Outputs go to `.scratch/icon-cycles-20260909/cycle-002/`. Six orthographic views expose support and tool connections. The lightboard rests on its base, with the brush beside it at the floor plane. The wool lies on a foam pad and the eye-free felting needle joins its grip and enters the fiber. Rays above the board are a flat emission cue, not physical scene geometry. Geometry findings are in [the retained review](geometry-review.json); [final hash-bound visual decisions](../../20260909-autonomous-cycle-002-visual.json) cover native sizes, light/dark backgrounds and the actual site renderer. Creator critical review was not independent.

The [Muse Lumify description](https://www.musepaintbar.com/public-events/lumify/) establishes illuminated shape guides on canvas; the icon is a generic illustrative lightboard, not a model-specific replica. [Brooklyn Craft Company's flat-felting workshop](https://www.brooklyncraftcompany.com/products/flat-felting-floral-bouquet-1) establishes forming raw wool with needles and finishing the work in a hoop. The needle is shown without sewing thread or eye. [Sparrows' hook-pick catalog](https://www.sparrowslockpicks.com/en-ca/products/hooker-lock-pick-set) and [L-shaped tension tools](https://www.sparrowslockpicks.com/products/level-two-tension-wrench-set) informed generic tool silhouettes; no brand imagery or mechanism instructions are reproduced. [Repair Café's description](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/about/) supports a broad community fix-it motif; the icon promises neither a particular repair service nor refreshments.

The collaborative picture uses separated canvases containing connected landscape portions and two brushes. The community repair design places a wrench and screwdriver beside a heart. These frontal diagrams do not imply complicated physical intersections. Needle-felting paint was simplified from 3,443 raw / 1,285 gzip bytes to 2,375 / 941 while retaining the projected outline and support.
