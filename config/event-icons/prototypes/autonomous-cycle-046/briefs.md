# Batch46 briefs

## Graham technique

Dance classes or demonstrations explicitly teaching Martha Graham technique, including contraction/release and floorwork.

Exclusions: Generic modern dance, Pilates, yoga, a specific choreography or guaranteed exercise instruction.

## Ballet barre

Ballet classes explicitly including barre work or programs centered on ballet-barre technique.

Exclusions: Generic ballet performances, pointe-specific training, fitness barre without ballet evidence or guaranteed equipment.

## Vogue

Voguing dance classes, practice or performances explicitly identified with the ballroom dance style.

Exclusions: Fashion exhibitions, magazine events, generic posing, a specific Vogue substyle or participant gender restriction.

## Knife skills

Culinary instruction explicitly teaching chef-knife handling, cutting or knife care.

Exclusions: Weapons, general cooking without knife instruction, specific recipe promises or a safe-handling demonstration.

## Swiss roll

Classes, tastings or food events explicitly centered on Swiss roll or rolled sponge cake with filling.

Exclusions: Unspecified baking, sushi, savory roulades, iced layer cakes or a guaranteed recipe, portion or dietary claim.
