# Batch 39 briefs

## Social needlework

Social groups explicitly welcoming multiple needlework or yarn techniques, such as knitting, crochet and hand needlework.

Exclusions: A single-technique class better served by its specific icon, mixed coloring and crafts, machine sewing alone or a promise of teaching or supplied materials.

## Graduate admissions fair

Events where prospective graduate students meet university admissions representatives, explore programs and discuss applications.

Exclusions: Graduation ceremonies, undergraduate-only fairs, ordinary academic lectures, guaranteed admission, scholarships or individualized paid admissions services.

## Museum discovery backpack

Museum programs lending activity backpacks or exploration kits for self-guided family discovery of art and exhibits.

Exclusions: School supply giveaways, emergency go bags, hiking trips, ordinary museum admission or a guarantee of a particular kit inventory.

## Structural color

Science workshops and interpretation explicitly about color produced by microscopic structures interacting with light.

Exclusions: Pigment mixing, dyeing, ordinary rainbows, a guaranteed microscope activity or treating all structural color as one exact optical mechanism.

## Musical chairs

Participatory musical-chairs games and events explicitly centered on the game.

Exclusions: Chair exercise, concerts with seated audiences, chair auctions, a specific chair layout, participant count or guaranteed prize.
