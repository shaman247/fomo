# Batch32 briefs

## Personal archiving

Organizing, cataloguing and preserving personal photographs, papers and ephemera, including digitization workshops.

Exclusions: Generic library shelving, genealogy research alone, cloud storage sales or a specific scanning device.

## Cross-stitch

Cross-stitch embroidery and decorating cloth or paper objects with explicit crossed stitches.

Exclusions: Generic embroidery, knitting, weaving, binding journals or a promised pattern or substrate.

## Pointed-pen calligraphy

Learning or practicing calligraphy using a pointed dip pen and ink.

Exclusions: Brush calligraphy, broad-edge scripts, ordinary handwriting or guaranteed exact kit contents.

## Film-location tour

Tours explicitly visiting places used as filming locations for movies or television.

Exclusions: Ghost folklore walks, ordinary screenings, filmmaking classes or guaranteed filming in progress.

## Paper-strip weaving

Interlacing broad strips of paper, foam paper or similar craft sheets into woven patterns.

Exclusions: Thread weaving on a frame loom, basketry, knitting or a traditional regional textile technique not actually taught.
