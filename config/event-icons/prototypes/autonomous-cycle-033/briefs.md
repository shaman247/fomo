# Batch33 briefs

## Harp

Music explicitly featuring harp, harp lessons and harp-led recitals.

Exclusions: Unspecified classical music, lute, lyre, or a promise of a particular pedal, lever or triple-harp model.

## Caviar

Explicit caviar tastings, caviar-focused meals or classes.

Exclusions: Generic seafood, sushi roe, wine tasting alone or a guaranteed species, brand, serving utensil or tin size.

## Recipe exchange

Sharing and exchanging recipes and their food memories.

Exclusions: Cooking classes, guaranteed prepared food, potlucks, ordinary writing or commercial recipe sales.

## Memoir writing

Writing personal memories and autobiographical literary stories.

Exclusions: General creative writing across genres, fiction-only classes, archival preservation or a spoken storytelling show.

## Makers’ market

Markets explicitly featuring independent makers, handmade goods and design.

Exclusions: Generic retail, produce farmers markets, beer tasting, or a guarantee of a depicted product/vendor.
