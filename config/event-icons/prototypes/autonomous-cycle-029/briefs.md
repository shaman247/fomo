# Batch 29 briefs

## Shadowcast performance

Live performers acting alongside a projected film as an explicit shadowcast.

Exclusions: Ordinary screenings, recorded theater, shadow puppets, or a promised production costume.

## Research focus group

Facilitated small-group research discussions gathering participant experiences and feedback.

Exclusions: Therapy, support groups, civic meetings or informal social conversation.

## Comedy news show

Written satirical or comic news performances.

Exclusions: Ordinary news talks, general stand-up, or all improv shows.

## Tree care

Hands-on care of street trees and tree beds, including weeding, soil care and mulching.

Exclusions: Tree identification walks, general gardening, timber cutting or an exact tree-care instruction diagram.

## 70mm film screening

Screenings explicitly presented on 70mm film.

Exclusions: Ordinary digital screenings, unspecified IMAX, or every widescreen movie.
