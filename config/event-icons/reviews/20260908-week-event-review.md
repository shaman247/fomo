The detailed genre inventory preserves **jazz, blues, house, R&B, hip-hop, reggaeton, Highlife, Afro-funk, ghazal, Sufi kalam, qawwali, candombe, chacarera, Manouche jazz, Jersey club, bouyon, konpa, old-time, Midwest emo**, and many narrower named styles. Music and dance remain different dimensions: tango music is not tango instruction; Afrobeat and Afrobeats are not spelling aliases. Playing techniques such as guitar tapping and slide guitar remain separate from instrument construction.

These genre concepts are **recognition experiments**. A color, arbitrary note shape, costume, national flag or generic instrument cannot be treated as a proven genre symbol. Prototype a small comparison set only after writing a distinguishing brief and test it beside neighboring genres. High event counts for jazz or house do not overcome that design uncertainty.
