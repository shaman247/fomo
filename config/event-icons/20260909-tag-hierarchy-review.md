## Applied decisions

- Indian Classical / Indian Classical Music / Hindustani Classical / Carnatic Music → tanpura. The unqualified Hindustani keyword stays unassigned because its sense is unverified.
- Language flags → speaking-head artwork; ordinary language learning does not inherit the reciprocal language-exchange icon.
- National cinema flags and food/religious shorthand → film clapperboard. Soviet Cinema includes Ukrainian-film evidence and is not equivalent to modern Russia.
- Art and broad cultural identity → art or neutral community artwork. Broad Indigenous and Asian American categories no longer use huts or Japanese dolls. Community artwork is interim, not a universal emblem for each culture.
- Improv, Book Club, Life Drawing, Karaoke, Tai Chi, Tarot and precise activities/instruments → their existing custom artwork.
- Blues/Pop/R&B/House/Jam and related wordplay → neutral music artwork pending a recognizable genre-specific design.
- Broad Chamber Music and Tabletop remain broad. Weaving, generic Pilates, Still Life, Stop Motion and Laser Cutting were not forced into narrower equipment/technique artwork.

**30 national-flag assignments replaced; seven retained:** American History, American Revolution, Founding Fathers, George Washington, Presidents Day, and the event/venue Revolutionary War tags. Their historical context supports the US reference; the modern flag is a national-context cue rather than a period-accurate artifact. All 18 other flags were retained, including pride symbols, tour-guide pennants and the competition flag.

The tag editor now validates explicit overrides against the full approved custom + Noto catalog. Existing emoji remain untouched as provenance/legacy fallback. No seed rerun, hierarchy edits, keyword promotion, event icon assignment or location icon assignment occurred. Tag topics can depict an instrument or technique without claiming it is the central activity of every event carrying that tag.
